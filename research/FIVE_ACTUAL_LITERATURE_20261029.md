# Targeted literature leads after SCC recognition

These are leads, not mathematical premises or novelty evidence. Only the cited
primary abstract pages were reviewed in this pass; no full-paper review is claimed.
The current advance is the project-specific application and its checked proof,
not a claim that implication graphs or SCC reasoning are new mathematics.

1. Alexandru Nica, “Free probability aspect of irreducible meander systems, and
   some related observations about meanders,” arXiv:1506.01345v3 (2015), journal
   publication2016: https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.01345 . The abstract relates
   irreducible meandric systems to the lattice NC(n), and their even generating
   function to the R-transform of a product of commuting independent centered
   semicircular variables. This is relevant to the existing connected-component
   expansion, but does NOT identify our pair/triple split families or our SCC
   survivor condition with those meandric systems.

2. Motohisa Fukuda and Ion Nechita, “Generating series and matrix models for
   meandric systems with one shallow side,” arXiv:2103.03615 (2021):
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.03615 . The abstract treats arch depth at most two
   on one side, uses free/Boolean moment-cumulant transforms, and obtains
   generating functions by enumerating irreducible objects. CONJECTURE/research
   lead: a shallow-certificate restriction might give a tractable subfamily in
   which to test SCC-filtered enumeration. No such bijection, formula, or
   extension to the full counting problem has been established.

A useful next test is an explicit encoding preserving the two split certificates,
class sizes, noncrossing and the SCC criterion, followed by comparison against
independent finite counts. Similar terminology or agreement at a few coefficients
would not prove equivalence. The previously killed Fuss-Catalan prediction must
not be revived solely from this analogy.

The earlier1999 Zhang–Yap–Jaffar primary source remains relevant to the constraint
framework: https://cdn.aaai.org/AAAI/1999/AAAI99-026.pdf . Its satisfiability results
do not themselves supply our Euler cancellation or survivor counting theorem.
