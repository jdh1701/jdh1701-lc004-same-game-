# Limits and rejected shortcuts

1. SCC alone is not an acceptance theorem in this mixed setting. It is used
   only as a necessary filter; the COMPLETE original family is always generated
   before accepting an object. Removing the filter reproduces full positive
   witness streams through j=4, and the initial unfiltered j=5 run agrees with
   the final filtered stream.
2. The all-five (1,2,2) argument extends to five-classes under a COMPLETE
   two-generator/full-join hypothesis. It does not establish that hypothesis
   for arbitrary mixed-profile survivors. Assuming it would circularly remove
   the very exceptional families still requiring proof.
3. The six-class was not restricted to the two observed run shapes. All22
   original local full-join pairs and all17 resulting compressed types were
   allowed, including adjacent six-class core positions. Finite zero branches
   do not prove that those branches are impossible for every j.
4. The 116857 constructed length-26 survivors do not establish a complete
   first-excess census. They combine a complete two-generator stratum with
   the independently proved three-run stratum. A nonzero pattern with no
   three-run and at least three certificates has not been excluded uniformly.
5. Positive replay and rotation unions check correctness and weighting.
   Absence of missed patterns additionally uses the paper completeness proof.
   The older complete n16/n21 censuses supply independent enumeration controls
   only at those lengths.

No coefficient sign or generator-count conjecture is used to prune the
original all-profile problem. This search explicitly solves only its stated
two-generator subproblem.
